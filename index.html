About

AIC Samutet is a vibrant community of faith dedicated to spreading the gospel, serving our neighbors, and fostering fellowship. Our mission is to bring people closer to God through worship, teaching, and community outreach.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of faith and hope in the community, inspiring lives through Christ-centered living.

Our Mission

To nurture spiritual growth, provide guidance, and serve those in need with love and compassion.

Our Values

Faith, Fellowship, Service, Integrity, and Community.
